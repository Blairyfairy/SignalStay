01 / CAMPAIGN DESIGN

Build around intent, not traffic.

For a fictional suite resort, the objective is confirmed direct reservations from domestic travelers. A proposed 30-day pilot uses a $100 daily ceiling; the example dataset below represents a separate illustrative observation window, not a full pilot.

Discover

City weekend ideas · resort neighborhoods · seasonal travel · local attractions · getaway planning

Broad or phrase discovery, with tightly controlled spend.

Compare

Premium city hotels · suite resorts · hotel amenities · resort comparisons · spacious rooms

Phrase match connects shoppers to relevant room and amenity pages.

Reserve

Book resort suite · direct hotel reservation · suite rates · room availability · weekend room offers

Exact and phrase match prioritize transaction-ready intent.

Research workflow

Compare five seed themes (city hotels, resort district, premium stays, suites, brand) in Google Trends using the same geography and period. Validate ten candidate queries in Keyword Planner for volume, bid ranges and competition before launch. Trends scores are relative interest, not search counts. No live research values are reproduced here.

Match strategy

Exact for booking terms; phrase for qualified comparisons; broad only with conversion tracking and search-term monitoring.

Negative keyword guardrails

Employment, internships, wallpapers, giveaways, hotel management courses. Review actual queries before applying negatives to avoid blocking legitimate travelers.

03 / OPTIMIZATION ROADMAP

Five decisions to improve efficiency.

01

Test responsive creative first

Compare format-level CPA and booking volume. Keep audiences and landing pages comparable; format alone does not establish causality.

02

Expand high-intent clusters

Move budget incrementally toward brand reservations and premium suite queries. Watch marginal CPA and impression share before scaling further.

03

Contain unproductive traffic

Giveaway and employment clusters show spend without bookings in this simulation. Review conversion lag and search terms, then pause or exclude irrelevant traffic.

04

Refine comparison traffic

Test tighter phrase variants and landing pages for resort comparisons. Avoid increasing bids until booking efficiency improves.

05

Validate assets and bidding

Test sitelinks for room types and booking policies, and callouts for verified amenities. Reconcile device totals and validate deduplicated booking events before testing conversion-focused Smart Bidding.

04 / MEASUREMENT GOVERNANCE

Evidence before scale.

Tracking

Use confirmed reservation IDs to deduplicate conversion events. Reconcile advertising and booking-system totals, account for cancellation and attribution lag.

Experiment

Predefine an acceptable CPA from booking economics. Compare matched test groups and monitor booking volume alongside CPA and conversion rate.

Reporting

Review query quality weekly and evaluate incremental bookings before reallocating spend. Separate observed metrics from proposed actions.
